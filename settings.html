Score

The feed is sorted by score, highest first. A score adds up the points below: three weights multiplied by numbers of days, and bonuses and penalties added on top. Every card's “Score” details show the points it got.

× points per day old

Days since the item was published, or since you added a link. Negative puts newer items first.

× points per day

For items you've opened. Positive means an item you just opened sinks, and climbs back as days pass. Clicking white also counts as opening.

× points per day

Days since you opened any post in the item's subscription, the same for all its posts. Positive means subscriptions you've left alone rise. Not for links, or subscriptions you've never opened anything from.

+ points

For items you've never opened.

+ points

For items you marked green.

+ points

For the post published right after the one you opened most recently in the same subscription, so you can follow a channel or playlist in order.

− points

For items you marked red. Make it bigger than everything else adds up to, and red items sink to the bottom.
