Score

The feed is sorted by score, highest first. An item starts at minus its age in days, so one point is one day: a bonus of 20 makes an item count as 20 days newer. Every card's “Score” details show the points it got.

+ points

For items you marked green.

+ points

For the post published right after the one you opened most recently in the same subscription, so you can follow a channel or playlist in order.

− points

For items you marked red. 100000 (about 270 years) puts them below everything else.

− points

For items you opened in the last few days (below). Clicking white also counts as opening.

days

How long the penalty lasts after you open an item. 0 turns it off.
